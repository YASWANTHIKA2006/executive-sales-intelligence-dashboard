Executive Sales Intelligence Dashboard

Overview

The Executive Sales Intelligence Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, product trends, regional performance, customer behavior, and strategic business insights. The dashboard helps organizations make data-driven decisions through visual analytics and KPI monitoring.

Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Track revenue trends over time.
* Identify top-performing products and salespersons.
* Evaluate sales channel effectiveness.
* Compare regional performance.
* Generate strategic insights for business decision-making.

Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* Microsoft Excel
* Data Visualization
* Business Intelligence
* KPI Analysis

---

Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Command Center

This page provides a high-level overview of business performance through:

* Total Revenue
* Units Sold
* Product Count
* Average Revenue
* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Revenue by Region
* Product Category Performance
* Interactive Region Filter

2. Sales Performance Dashboard

This page focuses on operational sales analysis:

* Total Sales
* Total Units Sold
* Average Unit Price
* Product Count
* Top 5 Products by Revenue
* Salesperson Performance
* Sales Channel Contribution
* Product Category Treemap

3. Strategic Intelligence Dashboard

This page provides management-level insights:

* Revenue by Region
* Sales Channel Contribution
* Product Category Performance
* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Interactive Slicers
* Strategic Business Analysis
